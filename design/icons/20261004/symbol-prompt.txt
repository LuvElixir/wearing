Use case: logo-brand. A professional identity concept comparison board for Wearing, personal AI agent, showing THREE beautifully designed minimal app symbols, A/B/C. NO mascot, no faces, no hands, no people. The lesson from excellent simple app identities is separation: expressive character inside app, a powerful simple brand symbol on the home screen. Do not recreate any existing logo. Retain Wearing's own cobalt #4562dc / pale blue #91a3ec / white palette. Pure clean flat vector-like construction, not sketchy or thin. No fabric textures, no clay render, no 3D device mockups. Strong silhouette at 24px, extremely economical geometry, balanced safe area.
Concept A: a distinctive lowercase-like W constructed from one broad, gently folded cobalt ribbon, two soft valleys, one short upturned fold at the right suggesting a rolled sleeve; smooth bold filled surfaces with one pale-blue reverse side, no round scribble line and no italic M. Wide compact silhouette, friendly yet capable, not sports logo or stock W font.
Concept B: a single simplified upturned shirt cuff abstracted to an open rounded band, short upright cobalt body with a broad pale blue lip turning out toward the viewer and one carefully placed open notch. It looks like a confidently rolled-up sleeve reduced to an emblem, no actual arm or clothing buttons. Elegant compact imperfect symmetry; not a cup, not a wallet, not a shopping bag. A simple two-plane fold.
Concept C: two broad soft interlocking folds, with a clean negative-space forward-opening gap; one solid cobalt band extends outward from a softer light-blue origin, suggesting another you moving into the world. Warm rounded geometry, just two meaningful forms, no infinity knot, no globe, no sparkle, no generic arrow triangle.
Place each centered at same scale on a white rounded-square app tile with very faint framing, one row three columns, neutral almost-white background and plenty of space. Small labels A/B/C below, no explanatory text, no Wearing wordmark. Flat editorial logo presentation, 1536x1024. Prioritize originality and optical finish.
