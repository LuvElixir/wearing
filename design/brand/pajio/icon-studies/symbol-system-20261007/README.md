# Pajio character symbol exploration

Status: direction study, not selected for production. Generated 2026-10-07 after the user rejected the full-body teddy icon candidates and requested study of Cue, ChatGPT dots and Grok Bot.

## Observed references

- Cue: the official website header uses a compact grey lobed character with eyes alongside a separate lowercase wordmark. Source: https://www.cue.im/ (visually inspected in browser).
- Dots: the announcement and help pages distinguish the product from selectable individual characters. The official Iggy example shows a compact pink, simple-faced character in a conversation header. Sources: https://openai.com/index/introducing-dots/ and https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001530-getting-started-with-your-dot (official example image visually inspected).
- Grok Bot: its design team reports that detailed clay/watercolor explorations lost clarity at sidebar scale. The chosen system uses consistent basic shapes, expressive eyes and controlled variants. Avatar movement communicates actual work state. Source: https://x.ai/news/designing-grok-bot (official written rationale; no claim of independently viewing all animation states).

These are design precedents, not evidence of Pajio market validation.

## Design inference

Separate the fixed application identity from optional character customization. Begin with a recognizable compact silhouette; keep the bear cue in two rounded ear contours and the pajama cue in a folded lapel. Avoid anatomy, plush microtexture, and detailed garment construction at icon scale. Build state changes around real work status rather than decorative perpetual animation.
