## 3. 行动轨迹

### 完整生成提示词

```text
Create one complete visual concept for an independently owned PERSONAL AI AGENT, using the working bilingual name Facet / 另一面. The user wants a narrative around the many aspects of oneself, a self expressed in another form, equipped with memory, purpose and the ability to act. It must clearly be an AGENT: it takes a casual idea, advances it autonomously within the user's intent and comes back with a result. Personal and mature; not a SaaS service, cute companion product, security brand or anonymous identity system.

CONCEPT: A LIVING TRACE — your intention continues moving.
Canvas 1600 x 1100 landscape. One cohesive concept only. No numbered option labels.

Exact brand text:
Facet
另一面
Personal Agent
Main Chinese line: 你的想法，有了下一步。
English support: A part of you, carrying things forward.

You are not one identity.
You have many facets.

DESIGN DIRECTION:
A warm white canvas #FBFAF6, bold black-brown #292624, rich muted petrol-teal #287D78 used very deliberately, one light warm-gray tint. Editorial and intelligent, minimal but full of character. Highly crafted typography: an elegant sturdy lightly flared Latin wordmark paired with an equally confident clean Chinese wordmark. No decorative calligraphy font. No tech jargon wallpaper.
The main symbol is a single expressive ink-like continuous path, loosely reminiscent of an f that has opened outward into a loop and a forward tail. It suggests both a person's distinctive gesture and an agent moving through a task. Give it a recognizably persistent base shape with two or three purposeful variations that show attention, action and returning. This can feel lively through posture and rhythm; do not add a cartoon face or make it a generic robot/mascot. The mark must remain clear at icon size. The two overlapping parts of its loop imply different facets of the same self, never a maze, lock, shield or fingerprint.
Show one hero mark in black and one smaller active version in teal with a short trailing line, genuinely related forms. Keep sophistication and humanity; avoid sterile cybersecurity and corporate AI platforms.
```
