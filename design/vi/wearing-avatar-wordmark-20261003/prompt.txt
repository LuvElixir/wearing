Use case: logo-brand.
Create ONE high quality, restrained brand-design sheet for "Wearing", a personal AI companion that acts for its owner. The sheet has two complementary deliverables: a very cute CHARACTER AVATAR and a bespoke "Wearing" WORDMARK. This is a thoughtful independent character brand, approachable, warm, comfortable, witty, graphically sophisticated.
Composition: horizontal canvas, nearly white flat background; upper-left one generous avatar, upper-right the beautifully drawn wordmark. Below, repeat this exact avatar at tiny size and the wordmark at header size to demonstrate clarity. No other text except "Wearing".
AVATAR: a charming head-and-shoulders portrait. Soft short ivory pebble-shaped face, round cheeks, very small two dark oval eyes, no mouth. The head tilts slightly, with a soft oversized cobalt garment enveloping the shoulders. One small ivory mitten peeks over the curved cuff, giving a shy but helpful "I'm here" personality. Compact silhouette; head is wider than tall; asymmetric relaxed pose. Beautifully continuous organic curves, 5 to 8 graphic shapes, solid colors, expertly composed negative space. It must be adorable rather than diagrammatic. No symmetrical triangular bowtie collar; no robot helmet, astronaut, hood, ears, antenna, smile, or rectangular long head.
WORDMARK: exact spelling "Wearing", capital W followed by lowercase earing. Hand-designed rounded geometric letterforms, substantial but not inflated, soft corners, elegant custom spacing. The W is a gentle folded-ribbon form in cobalt, and "earing" is dark ink, with a distinctive simple single-storey a and g. The small i dot echoes the avatar's oval eyes. Prefer compact, quietly memorable, contemporary independent brand lettering. No extra mascot face in the wordmark. No trailing period. Show the relation between folded fabric and W subtly without sacrificing letter recognition.
Palette: rich cobalt blue, warm pale ivory, very dark ink; one lighter blue accent if essential. Entire result must be flat clean vector-like design, no photorealism, gradient, shadow, mockup, grain, labels, device UI, or decorative extras. This is a new attractive composition, not a technical icon reconstruction.
