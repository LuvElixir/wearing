# A little person, not a video player

A tap is answered by an actual small head-and-mitten gesture. Wearing has time to notice, respond, and settle. The surface never moves or squashes the entire bitmap and never invents spoken reply bubbles.

Each action shares one resting cuff pose and ends there. Attention is a small acknowledgement, listening is an inquisitive head turn, thinking is a thoughtful upward glance and mitten-to-chin pose. Quiet presence is a breath and weight settle. Waiting offers an open mitten before relaxing; working tightens the cuff with determination. These carry temperament, not claims about completed work.

Actual run / input / handoff states select footage. Idle footage plays occasionally with quiet holds between; click responses are one-shot, repeated clicks do not stack. New footage is decoded before swapping, using the common anchor. Reduced-motion gets a still image. Hidden and off-screen media pause. No play icon, pause icon, fake progress, or video scrubber appears.
