# Treatment

A small presence beside the conversation. Wearing stands comfortably, notices the user, gives a tiny head tilt and relaxed mitten-hand hello, and settles back into the exact starting pose. One clear small response, not constant bouncing. Soft fabric and rounded proportions match the chosen character. Camera, lighting, body scale, and white background stay fixed; all limbs remain visible. Breathing and a single blink supply life. A six-second loop can be paused for reduced motion or via a visible control.

The image is a production asset, isolated from the reference board; the board's copy, interface and extra poses are not part of the generated scene. The app moves from an intimate character welcome into a continuous conversation, with the same presence beside messages. Actual results, approvals and connection needs appear within that conversation.

## User direction refinement

The user clarified that sleeve-rolling means ready to act, so the final image holds the opposite cuff. The loop is one subtle cuff tug, a small confident nod and a blink, returning to the cuff-holding pose. No wave. Hero copy: “这件事，我来。” The first standalone neutral-standing image is retained as production history, not the final chosen pose.
