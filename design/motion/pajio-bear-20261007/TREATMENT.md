# Treatment

A quietly attentive soft toy. Keep the camera and soles anchored. A single glance, slow blink and small paw greeting establish attention; the head and body articulate independently, the striped fabric follows the shoulder. No talking or dramatic grin. White studio ground isolates performance for compositing; preserve the soft facial design and little sewn mouth.

Wardrobe interaction uses a short dressing-curtain transition while assets become resident, normalized by actual character bounds. The generated changing sample tests whether a turn plus cuff adjustment can carry the clothing transformation convincingly; do not replace all eight outfits until actual footage demonstrates continuity.
